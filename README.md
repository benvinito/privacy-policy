# privacy-policy
SMOWI is a comprehensive, online proctoring and identity verification platform developed to safeguard the integrity, security, and credibility of digital assessments.

. Introduction

SMOWI is an online proctoring and identity verification platform developed to protect the integrity, security and credibility of digital assessments. This Privacy Policy explains how SMOWI collects, uses, stores and protects user information when using the SMOWI Chrome Extension and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the assessment and the features enabled, SMOWI may collect the following information:

Personal information, such as name, email address and student identification details.

Identity verification information.

Webcam images and video recordings for assessment monitoring.

Microphone information when audio monitoring is required.

Browser activity and assessment-related information.

Device and system information.

Assessment activity, timestamps and possible security incidents.

3. How We Use Information

SMOWI uses collected information to:

Verify the identity of students before assessments.

Monitor online examinations.

Detect suspicious activity and possible examination misconduct.

Protect the integrity and security of digital assessments.

Generate assessment monitoring reports.

Help educational institutions review examination results and incidents.

4. Camera and Microphone Permissions

SMOWI may request access to the camera and microphone when required for online examination monitoring and identity verification. These permissions are used only for the assessment-related features that require them.

5. Browser and Device Permissions

The extension may access certain browser and device information to support secure assessments, monitor examination activity and identify possible security risks. Access is limited to what is necessary for the platform's functionality.

6. Data Storage and Security

SMOWI uses appropriate security measures to protect collected information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Information is stored and processed through the platform's authorised systems.

7. Sharing of Information

SMOWI does not sell personal information. Relevant assessment and verification information may be shared with authorised educational institutions, examination administrators and service providers when necessary to provide the proctoring service.

8. Data Retention

Personal information and assessment records are retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet institutional requirements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. User Privacy and Control

Users are informed when permissions are required for assessment monitoring. Users should review the permissions requested by the extension before using the platform.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SMOWI may update this Privacy Policy when its services, features or data practices change. Updated versions will be made available through the appropriate platform or website.
